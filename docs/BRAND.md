# Visual identity

<img src="../apps/web/public/brand/catalogforge-mark.png" alt="CatalogForge mark" width="120" height="120" />

The mark combines catalog cards into a C, with an amber tab against a blue background. The application renders the product name as text for legibility and accessibility.

- **Asset:** [`catalogforge-mark.png`](../apps/web/public/brand/catalogforge-mark.png), 1254 × 1254 PNG with a transparent outer background.
- **Component:** [`BrandMark.vue`](../apps/web/src/components/BrandMark.vue), used in login, navigation, loading and product presentation. The image is decorative when accompanied by the product name.
- **Palette:** blue `#215ACB`, amber `#F2AE45`, dark text `#172945` and white.
- **Usage:** preserve square proportions and internal spacing. Prefer display sizes of at least 24 pixels; do not stretch or add a border.

The same asset is used for the favicon, home-screen icon and README.
